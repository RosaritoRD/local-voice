# 💸 Savings and tips

## Spend less

1. **No per-character cost.** Cloud voice services charge for every character. Here each audio file costs 0: you only pay for the server you already have.
2. **Use Piper for drafts and Kokoro for the final version.** Piper is about 2× faster; you don't need the best voice to review a script.
3. **Set `OMP_NUM_THREADS` to your CPU count.** With more threads than cores, audio comes out slower, not faster.
4. **Mix it with the cloud.** Use local voice for long videos or tests, and a paid voice only where it matters most — for example, the first seconds of a Short.
5. **No internet access.** The container doesn't need a network: less attack surface and no traffic to pay for.

## Do it better

1. **Speed 0.88–0.92** for prayers and meditations. Faster sounds like an ad.
2. **Write for the ear:** short sentences and one paragraph per idea. The engine adds a pause between paragraphs.
3. **Compare before you switch.** If you replace a paid voice with a local one on a channel, measure retention for a week before deciding: a flatter voice can cost views.
4. **Ideas to extend it:** voice cloning with an engine that supports it, a job queue for very long texts, or a `TTS_PROVIDER=local` option in your video app.
